🔍 CASE 001 • EVIDENCE BOARD

What IS Artificial Intelligence?

Study the evidence carefully, Detective. AI is not magic , it's very clever maths.

🔬 DETECTIVE'S DEFINITION:

AI is a computer program that learns from thousands of examples and finds patterns , just like a detective studying clues. Show it 1,000 cat photos and 1,000 dog photos, and it learns to tell them apart. No feelings. No magic. Just very fast pattern-matching.

✅ AI CAN:
  • Recognise faces & voices
  • Translate 100+ languages
  • Write stories & poems
  • Sort millions of photos
  • Recommend music & shows
  • Answer questions (sometimes wrong!)
❌ AI CANNOT:
  • Have real feelings
  • Hug a sad friend
  • Taste or smell anything
  • Be 100% right , ever!
  • Replace a real human being
  • Think creatively like YOU do
🧠 HUMAN vs 🤖 AI

🔍 CASE 005 • DETECTIVE TOOLKIT

Expose the Liar , Use the CHECK Framework!

When AI says something, a good detective never just believes it. Use CHECK to investigate!

C
Check it carefully
Read the AI answer slowly. Does it answer your exact question?
H
Hold on , does it sound right?
Use your own knowledge. Does this make sense? Could it be wrong?
E
Explore another source
Open a book, encyclopedia or trusted website. Check it there.
C
Compare the answers
Do they match? If they don't , AI got it wrong!
K
Keep the verified answer
Use only what you've confirmed is TRUE. Tell an adult if you're unsure.
⚠️ AI SAID: 'The Great Wall of China is visible from space' , FALSE! | 'Sharks are fish' , TRUE! | 'Napoleon was short' , MYTH! (He was average height!)

🔍 CASE 007 • SAFETY SYSTEMS TEST

STOP , THINK , TELL: The Detective's Traffic Light

Every time you use AI, run through the traffic light. It takes 5 seconds and keeps you safe!

🛑 STOP!
Before you type ANYTHING , pause for 3 seconds.

• Am I about to share personal information?
• Does an adult know I'm using AI right now?
• Would I be happy if Mum/Dad could see this?
If the answer to any of these is NO , STOP and check first.
🤔 THINK!
Now think about what you're doing:

• Is this question safe and appropriate?
• Will the answer be true? (Remember , CHECK it!)
• Could this information hurt me or someone else?
If in doubt , don't send it yet. Think some more.
📢 TELL!
After using AI:

• Tell a trusted adult what you used AI for today.
• If AI said something strange or worrying , tell someone.
• If AI asked for your personal details , tell IMMEDIATELY!
Telling an adult is always the right move.
